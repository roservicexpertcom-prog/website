How Frequently Your RO Filters Needs To Be Replaced: Pre and post Carbon filter – 6-8 months, Spun filter -3-4 months, Sediment filter – 6-8 months, Membrane – 10-12 months. This frequency is only ideal where water is not very hard. If the water is hard (TDS value>2000) – then life for each of the part is reduced to 50-60% and thus time interval needed for replacement of these parts also halves.
